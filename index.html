The Bottleneck

Verification doesn't scale with headcount — it scales with fatigue.

Volume outpaces reviewers

Batch records and qualification packets arrive faster than any QA team can read them line by line.

Cross-referencing is manual

Every clause has to be matched against a regulation by hand, then matched again after revisions.

Fatigue becomes risk

A missed inconsistency on page 40 of a batch record can mean a delayed release or a 483 observation.

The Solution

Every record, checked against the rule that governs it.

Telomara acts as an Augmented Review Assistant. It reads documents line by line, maps them to regulatory clauses (21 CFR, EU MDR, ISO), and flags gaps.

  • Runs on-premise. Nothing leaves your network.
  • Traces every finding. No black-box AI scoring.

Vision

One discipline, mapped across every domain that needs it.

We believe rigor doesn't have to stay in the lab. The same standard of evidence that governs a batch record can govern a diagnosis, a power grid, or a city plan — if the AI reasoning it every step of the way is built to be checked, not just trusted.

Mission

How we build, stated as plainly as we'd want it audited.

Our team's roots are in bench science and regulated quality systems. We hold applied AI to the same standard — evidence, traceability, and a human who stays in charge of the decision.

01 PRINCIPLE · EVIDENCE

Ground every model in verifiable domain science
