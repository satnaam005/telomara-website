AI-Powered Regulatory Compliance Software for Life Sciences.

Telomara is an on-premise software platform that automates the verification of pharmaceutical batch records and QMS documents. It acts as an Augmented Review Assistant: reading documents line by line, mapping them to regulatory clauses, and flagging gaps so your team can resolve exceptions faster. AI suggestions are advisory; qualified humans retain GxP decision authority.

On-premise / offline-ready pilot software. Not marketed as a fully validated automated release system or a certified 21 CFR Part 11 substitute.

The bottleneck

Verification doesn't scale with headcount — it scales with fatigue.

Volume outpaces reviewers

Batch records, SOPs, and qualification packets arrive faster than any QA team can read them line by line.

Cross-referencing is manual

Every clause has to be matched against a regulation by hand, then matched again after the next revision.

Fatigue becomes risk

A missed inconsistency on page 40 of a batch record can mean a delayed release — or a 483 observation.

The approach

One engine that helps your team check documents against your rules.

Telomara ingests your regulatory library once — CFR sections, ICH guidelines, internal SOPs — and keeps it current. Every document you upload after that is checked against the full set automatically, with each finding traced back to its source clause.

  • Runs on your infrastructure. Nothing leaves your network — batch records stay on-premise, always.
  • Traces every finding. No black-box scoring — each flag links to the exact clause it violates.
  • Fits your review process. Reviewers confirm or override findings — Telomara narrows the pile, it doesn't replace sign-off.

The pipeline

Four steps, in order, every time.

Each document moves through the same sequence, so what you see in the review queue is always reproducible.

01 — INGEST

Upload the document

Batch records, SOPs, or qualification packets, in the formats you already use.

02 — MAP

Match clause to clause

Each section is aligned to the specific regulation or SOP requirement it's meant to satisfy.

03 — VERIFY

Check for gaps

Missing signatures, out-of-range values, and outdated references are flagged with their source.

04 — REPORT

Hand off a clean queue

Reviewers see only what needs a human decision, with the full trace attached.
